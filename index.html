FAQ

Is Phold free?

Yes. Six cameras and their film cost nothing.

What's premium?

The Phold-75, Reflex Twin and Plastique 120: one free shot each, then $1.99 to keep, or $4.99 for Unlock all.

Why wait a day?

That's film. If you can't wait, Develop now brings a roll back at once.

How do I get prints?

Add them when you load the film, or pick them from a roll after. A lab posts them to you.

Which countries?

The app is everywhere except China, Russia and Belarus. Prints post to more than 100 countries.

When do prints arrive?

Usually within two weeks. The app shows where they've got to.

Can I get a refund?

For App Store purchases, ask Apple. For prints, email me: see the terms.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later. Best on iPhone Duo.

Do you keep my photos?

No. They stay on your phone unless you order prints. Privacy policy.
